## A1. Ship gridding (Text S1.3)

**Ready to paste.** Ship swath from NCEI was gridded with MB-System 5.8.2beta06 from the `docker.io/mbari/mbsystem` image (digest `sha256:106f502d9f97ee0e9f5d889726320d1ae73a95fb102069e357a0bb0b35b39668`), run as an Apptainer sandbox; every products record carries this software block. For the development pairs acquired after the review and for all eight blind test 2 sites the raw swath files were gridded without cleaning, editing or `mbprocess`: the files were decompressed and listed in a datalist, a footprint depth was taken as the median |z| of the first 300,000 soundings returned by `mblist -OXYZ` over the grid region, and `mbgrid` was called once per cruise as `mbgrid -I <datalist> -O <grid> -R <w/e/s/n> -E <dx>/<dy>/degrees -A2 -G3 -F1 -C0 -M`: bathymetry as topography (positive up), Gaussian-weighted mean (`-F1`, weight A exp(−r²/a²) with a = half the grid spacing, scale factor 1, the default), no spline gap filling (`-C0`), GMT netCDF output (`-G3`), with the standard-deviation and count grids also written (`-M`). No search radius, clipping or extension flag was set. The cell size follows the beam footprint at the footprint depth, 2 × depth × tan(beam width / 2), with the beam width taken from the sonar family (1.0° for Kongsberg EM 120/122/124/300/302/304/710/712/2040 and Reson; 2.0° for SeaBeam 2100/2112 and EM 1002; 3.3° for SeaBeam 2000; 1.5° for SeaBeam 3012/3050/1050 and as the default; 2.3° for Hydrosweep), converted to degrees of longitude and latitude at the region's mean latitude. The grid region is the union of the AUV footprint parts chosen by the region rule (the whole union, or the largest part plus every part within 5 km of it when the parts cover less than 20 % of their bounding box) padded by 1 km; the grid is then reprojected to the pair's UTM zone with GDAL's default output lattice. The native ship cell used in the analysis (`lr_native`) is the larger of this beam footprint at the pair's median ship depth and the grid posting. Eight development pairs from the June 2026 work (RR1506, AT37-13, TN399 (MGDS 30373), EW9801, NA090, NA076, NA080 (MGDS 31290), EW0207) were gridded by the same command and flags over the union of their AUV footprints padded by 1 km, also raw. Four June pilot pairs (AR26, TN299 (both grids), FK181031 (MGDS 24618)) differ: their cruises had at most 100 swath files and were passed through `mbdatalist -Z`, `mbclean` with default settings and `mbprocess` before gridding, over the AUV footprint bounding box padded by 1 km, with the footprint depth taken from the AUV catalog. The provider grids (DISCOL, CCZ, TAG, Cal DIG) were not gridded here; their ship products were binned from the providers' EM 122 swath (DISCOL, CCZ, TAG) and are unavailable for Cal DIG, which has no swath.

## A2. Contract v2.1 products (Text S1.3)

**Ready to paste.** The ship-side product rasters (interface contract v2.1) are binned from the swath soundings onto the pair's ship grid lattice (same CRS, transform, shape and cell as `lr.tif`), using `mblist -F<format> -I <file> -MA -R <window> -O XYZDG,AFNM` over the ship grid's bounds padded by the larger of 2 km and three times the median depth, and the floor of each sounding's projected (x, y) onto the lattice. Five products are available to the analysis: `ship_count`, the number of soundings in the cell (NaN where zero); `ship_sd`, the standard deviation of the soundings in the cell (n − 1 denominator; cells with at least two soundings; meters); `ship_rsd`, the robust standard deviation 1.4826 × median |z − median z| (at least three soundings; meters); `ship_xtrack_frac`, the mean across-track position of the soundings as a fraction of the ping half-width, where the half-width is the largest |across-track distance| among the ping's valid beams (0 to 1); and `ship_beam_angle`, the mean |beam angle from vertical| in degrees, taken from the launch angle reported by `mblist` when it is valid for at least 99 % of soundings and otherwise from the geometry atan(|across-track| / |depth|). Two further rasters, `ship_mean_regrid` and `ship_median_regrid` (meters, positive up), serve only the registration test. The 14 window features used by the predictors are seven statistics of these products over the native ship cells of each window, computed at two window sizes (128 and 224 AUV pixels, suffixes `_128` and `_224`): `rsd_med` and `rsd_p90` (median and 90th percentile of `ship_rsd`), `sd_med` (median of `ship_sd`), `logcount_med` (median of log10 `ship_count` over cells with soundings), `xtrack_mean` (mean of `ship_xtrack_frac`), `beam_mean` (mean of `ship_beam_angle`) and `rsd_over_sd_med` (median of `ship_rsd` / `ship_sd` over cells with `ship_sd` > 0); all are unweighted statistics over the cells of the window, which has max(2, ceil(window / ratio)) cells on a side with ratio = ship cell / AUV pixel, and all are NaN for a pair whose products are not usable. Products are usable for a pair when they were built (which requires a recoverable k) and the registration test against the ship grid raises none of three flags: `insufficient_overlap` (fewer than 500 common cells); `vertical_offset` (|median of `ship_median_regrid` − `lr.tif`| larger than max(0.5 m, 0.05 × the pair's median `s_lr`)); and `registration_shift`, raised when the robust standard deviation of the difference, evaluated on a grid of shifts from −1 to +1 cell in steps of 0.25 cell (bilinear sampling, 81 positions), reaches its minimum at a shift of at least 0.5 cell in x or y and the gain σ(0, 0) − σ(argmin) is at least max(0.10 m, 0.05 × σ(0, 0)). The `ship_xtrack_frac` features of EX1402L2 are treated as unavailable by a consumer-side override.

## A3. Co-registration (Text S2.1)

**Ready to paste (estimator).** The AUV grid was co-registered to the ship grid by a rigid shift (dx, dy, dz) found by a two-stage grid search that minimizes the median absolute deviation of the residuals between the ship grid, sampled bilinearly at the shifted positions of up to 80,000 randomly drawn valid AUV pixels (seed 0), and the AUV elevations. The coarse search covers ±30 m in both axes at a step of max(4 × AUV pixel, 2 m); the fine search covers ± one coarse step around the coarse minimum at a step of max(AUV pixel, 0.5 m); there is no sub-pixel interpolation beyond the fine step. The vertical shift dz is the median of the residuals at the selected horizontal shift, and the post-fit misfit is the median absolute deviation of the residuals about dz. The shift is applied as a coordinate translation of the AUV grid (no resampling of its values). Because the coarse grid extends to the last step at or beyond 30 m, shifts larger than 30 m can be and were found (for example 47 to 54 m for several blind test 2 sites), and these are recorded.

**Ready to paste (harmonizing).** Each grid was reprojected with bilinear resampling to the pair's UTM zone (the zone of the center of the overlap of the two bounding boxes) on GDAL's default output lattice, which preserves the native ground sampling distance, and clipped to the overlap of the two valid-data polygons. The AUV pixel is therefore the native pixel for the pairs built after the review (rule "native per side"; the AUV grid is never resampled coarser), and the ship pixel is the default warp of the geographic `mbgrid` posting (29.4 m becomes 29.36 m for AR26). Two groups differ. The 18 Cal DIG pairs were harmonized under an approved addendum to a 2 m AUV grid (single warp to EPSG:26910, area averaging for input finer than 2 m, cubic for 2 m input) "for a uniform 5× ratio against the 10 m LR". TN159, a lockbox pair built in June 2026 from three AUV tiles, has a 3.25 m pixel: the June mosaic code caps multi-tile mosaics at about 3,000 pixels per side, `res = max(native, span / 3000)`; no report or ledger entry states this (inference from `src/discovery/stage_f_harmonize.py:101-115` and `logs/sbatch_31525165.err:72`, "MGDS:21981 tiles=3"). Other as-used pixels that differ slightly from the native value (discol 2.19 m, tag 1.89 m, EW0207 1.23 m, NA090, NA076 and FK181031 about 1.05 m) come from the same default-lattice warp.

**Ready to paste (QA thresholds).** A pair passed the frozen co-registration QA if the horizontal shift was at most 30 m, the overlap of the valid polygons was at least 1 km², and at least one 256-pixel tile on the stride-256 lattice of the AUV grid was at least 50 % joint-valid, where joint-valid means AUV finite and ship real (the ship validity mask averaged onto the AUV grid at least 0.5, then eroded by one pixel). The 30 m bound is the documented absolute accuracy of the Cal DIG AUV USBL navigation, proposed in the June co-registration addendum and adopted in the configuration. No residual-misfit threshold is part of this gate: the post-fit median absolute deviation is recorded only (the June stage-F QA had used 5 m, and the configuration still lists 5 m, but neither was used by the post-review gate). For blind test 2 the 30 m bound was replaced (ruling of 2026-10-14) by a local test: the pre-correction joint-valid bounding box is split into 2 × 2 quadrants, quadrants failing the area and tile rule are merged with a neighbor along their longer side (smallest first), each group is co-registered with the frozen estimator, and the pair is rigid if every group's shift agrees with the global shift within one ship cell; otherwise each group moves by its own residual rounded to whole AUV pixels with its own dz (drift case), tiles crossing a group boundary are set invalid, and the pair passes only if every group passes the QA. Blind test 2 also required an AUV pixel in [0.985, 4.99] m, 3.88 to 51.2 ship cells across a 256-pixel window, and the datum test G ≤ T_G, where G is the 95th percentile of |AUV − ship| (AUV area-averaged onto the ship lattice over the joint-valid ship cells, after removing the median difference) divided by the median ship depth, and T_G = 0.200712 is three times the largest G among the 45 development-designated pairs (0.066904, 2009_Amundsen (MGDS 30047)).

## A4. k sweep (Text S3.2)

**Ready to paste.** The recoverable fine-band factor k of a pair was chosen from the candidates 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32. Both grids were resampled bilinearly onto a common UTM lattice at max(0.5 m, AUV pixel) over the AUV extent (center-cropped to at most 1,024 pixels per side), plane-detrended and Hann-windowed, and the radial power spectral density of the AUV grid was estimated in 48 logarithmically spaced bins. For each k the target frequency f_t = k / lr_native was formed; a k is unresolvable if f_t exceeds 0.9 of the Nyquist frequency. The local log-log slope of the AUV spectrum over a half-octave window around f_t (at least three bins) gives the verdict: `roughness_only` if the slope is at least −0.8 (white-like), `circular_leak` if the magnitude-squared coherence between the ship and AUV grids in the three bins nearest 1 / lr_native is at least 0.136, `recoverable_signal` if the slope is at most −0.97, and `marginal` otherwise. The pair's k is the largest candidate with verdict `recoverable_signal` (not the largest contiguous one; for EW9801 the verdicts were recoverable at 2, roughness only at 4 and recoverable at 8, giving k = 8). The three thresholds were calibrated on the 21 provider-validated pairs plus white-noise and circular controls derived from DISCOL: −0.97 is the largest anchor slope at k = 2 (−1.02) plus 0.05; −0.8 is the mean of that largest anchor slope and the white-control slope (−0.59); 0.136 is the mean of the 90th-percentile anchor coherence and the circular-control coherence (0.267). All 21 anchors are recoverable at k = 2. The sweep returns status `ok` when a spectrum could be formed (k may still be absent when no candidate is recoverable) and `no_curve` when the AUV grid had fewer than 64 finite cells in the cropped window; `missing_grid` and `load_failed` cover absent or unreadable files. No status named "sweep" exists: "sweep" is a value of the `k_source` field, which says whether k came from the sweep or from the June record. Six development candidates had no k and were not scored: TN268 (MGDS 30466), KIWI10RR (MGDS 24499) and EX1202L2 (MGDS 20815) with `no_curve`; FK181031 (MGDS 24367) and both 2009_Amundsen pairs (MGDS 30046, 30047) with `ok` but no recoverable candidate. The sweep lives entirely in this repository; the analysis repository reads k from the manifest and scores only pairs that have one.
