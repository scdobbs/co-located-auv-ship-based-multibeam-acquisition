## 2. Challenge clause — wrong or underspecified instructions, with evidence

1. **Report convention.** The repo's convention is `reports/stage_<x>_report_<date>.md`, but `reports/` is gitignored (`.gitignore`: "Reports folder excluded from git"), so a report there cannot be "committed". The steering instance placed the directive in the new, untracked `reports_post_grl_review/`; this report and all ACQ-R01 artifacts live in `reports_post_grl_review/ACQ-R01/` and are committed. Bulk outputs stay on OAK/scratch.
2. **Contract path for Cal DIG.** The contract puts products at `harmonized/<pair_id>/ship_products_v1/`. The 18 Cal DIG pairs are stored as `harmonized/cal_dig_morro_bay/<sub-site>/` (manifest `harmonized_path_*`), not by `pair_id`. Products (here only `products.json`, `available: false`) are written next to each pair's `lr.tif`, i.e. in the pair's actual harmonized directory; consumers must resolve the directory from the manifest path, not from `pair_id`. This is a v1 contract wording issue, not a change.
3. **"Fetch the swath files listed in the persisted fetch plan … exactly as stage C used them."** The plan (`stage_b_file_plan_2026-06-22.csv`) also lists `.fnv` nav sidecars (most 0-byte at NCEI); stage C never used them (it decompressed the swath and let MB-System regenerate ancillary files). ACQ-R01 fetched the swath files only (same subset / whole mode per cruise). One cruise-level nuance: for FK181031, NA080, TN299, TN399 and 2009_Amundsen the June subset was cut for the *then-live* HR ids (e.g. FK181031 for MGDS:24367;24620); the pairs now served (24618, 31253, 30046, …) were harmonized from the same subset grids, so the subset is the correct one.
4. **"R03 found `raw_lr/` and `raw_lr_gridded/` empty."** Confirmed, and it is broader: essentially everything under `$GROUP_SCRATCH/auv_ship_colocated_bathy/` was purged around 2026-09-20 (90-day rule) — `raw_lr/` 38 empty dirs, `raw_lr_gridded/` empty, `staging_phase1/` 0 files, `raw/` 0 files, `reharvest_c1/` 0 files, `qc_plots/` empty, `harmonized/` 1 `hr.tif` left of 44 dirs (§3.2). OAK is the only copy of every harmonized product and of the 4 provider raw grids. Nothing that the manifest points to is lost (all 39 pairs' `hr/lr/masks/footprint` are on OAK, verified by opening every non-lockbox `lr.tif`).
5. **"About 73 acquirable HR (27 + 18 + 28)".** The last gate (`stage_a_gate_20260605_v3.md`) reported 85 acquirable HR (31 training + 19 eval-only + 35 raw_lr_to_grid); 85 − 10 manifest-seed duplicates − 1 quarantined − 1 escalated = 73. The reconciliation (§3.3) is done on all 85.
6. **§2.4 tile footprints.** The R03 per-tile table keys (`<pid>_r<r0>_c<c0>.npz`) are the R02 `extract_clean_patches.py` windows, which are **256 × 256 HR px** (`--hr_px 256`; eval stride 128), not the "128 × 128" that R04 §1.3 states for the same tiles. The beam-geometry test uses the 256-px windows because that is what the R03 T_res / T_res_lp values were computed on. Flagged for the CNN repo, not changed here.
7. **§3 "candidate pairs by tier".** The June gate's tiers are ratio bands (training 5–25×, eval 25–40×, excluded > 40×, raw_lr_to_grid = ratio unknown) that the k-standard later superseded (deep pairs at ratio 40–80× are trainable at k = 8–16). Counts are reported by gate tier *and* by the empirical k prior; neither can measure k without grids, as the directive anticipates.
8. **§2.2 step 6 "USGS Cal DIG I source data".** The Cal DIG I ship mosaic (10.5066/P9QQZ27U) is a USGS composite of six sources: NOAA hydrographic surveys H13091, H13151, H13152 (Rainier), W00479 (Fairweather), R/V Sally Ride SR1604 (gap fill) and the 1998 Ocean Alert EM300 survey. NCEI distributes the NOS surveys as BAG grids (`H13151/BAG/*.bag`), not per-beam swath; only SR1604 and the EM300 survey exist as raw swath and they cover a minority of the mosaic. Per-cell sounding statistics of the *mosaic* cannot be reconstructed, so Cal DIG is `available: false` (§4.3).

### 4.2 Build (NCEI cruises + provider raw)

`build_products.py`, per pair: `mblist -F<fmt> -I <file> -MA -R w/e/s/n -O "XYZDG,AFNM"` on every swath file (window = `lr.tif` bounds in EPSG:4326 padded by max(2 km, 3 × median depth)), MB-System 5.8.2beta06 in the pinned Apptainer sandbox, **raw** mode (stage C gridded raw: C-full-0 spot check). Field codes verified against the installed man page (`/usr/local/share/man/man1/mblist.1`): `X` lon, `Y` lat (beam positions with `-MA`), `Z` topography positive up (sign as `lr.tif`), `D` bathymetry acrosstrack distance (m), `G` flat-bottom grazing angle, `,A` beam depression angle measured from vertical down (the launch angle, includes refraction), `F` beam flag (0 = good; `-MA` outputs valid beams only), `N` ping count, `M` unix time. Half-width per ping = max |D| over the ping's valid beams (per file, ping = `N`). Coordinates EPSG:4326 → `lr.tif` CRS with pyproj/PROJ (the library rasterio used in stage F), floor-binned onto the `lr.tif` transform; count, mean (offset by the pair's median depth for precision), SD (n ≥ 2), mean |D|/half-width, mean beam angle (`,A` where the format carries it for ≥ 99 % of beams, else geometric atan(|D|/|Z|) — recorded per pair). Rasters float32/NaN on the identical grid (re-opened and compared to `lr.tif`: CRS, transform, shape). `products.json` per contract; `products_extra.json` (same dir) carries per-file counts, window, method, s_lr source. Exact command lines are in `products.json → commands`.
