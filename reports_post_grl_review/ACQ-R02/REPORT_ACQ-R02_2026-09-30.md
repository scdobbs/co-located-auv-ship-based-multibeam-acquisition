### Other sources (no harvest)

- **Ifremer / SISMER (France).** Ifremer's AUV bathymetry (AsterX, IdefX; e.g. Lucky Strike, MoMAR, Nice slope) and the ship multibeam of the French fleet (Pourquoi pas?, L'Atalante, Marion Dufresne; EM122/EM710/EM302) are catalogued by SISMER at data.ifremer.fr / sextant.ifremer.fr, with campaign DOIs (`doi.org/10.17600/<campaign>`) and a Sextant CSW/OGC catalogue; raw multibeam is distributed on request through the SISMER "données brutes" service rather than by open URL, and AUV grids are typically released as campaign products under an Ifremer licence. Access is therefore a per-campaign request (SEANOE for open products, SISMER for raw swath), not a bulk API; it fits ACQ-R03-style targeted requests, not this harvest.
- **NOC / BODC Autosub (UK).** Autosub6000 / Autosub Long Range EM2040 bathymetry from NERC cruises (JC0xx, DY0xx, JR…) is archived at BODC (bodc.ac.uk, NERC Data Catalogue, DOI'd "published data sets") together with the ship EM120/EM122 swath of RRS James Cook / Discovery held by BODC and the MBBDB-like NERC Vessel data; discovery is through the BODC catalogue search and the UK Marine Environmental Data & Information Network (MEDIN) discovery metadata, download by registered request. Several NOC AUV surveys (Whittard Canyon, Haig Fras, Darwin Mounds) have ship EM120 companions on the same cruise, i.e. DISCOL-style pairs; they need per-dataset BODC requests.
- **JAMSTEC (Japan).** JAMSTEC's DARWIN data site (darwin.jamstec.go.jp) and the JAMSTEC Data and Sample Research System expose R/V Kaimei/Yokosuka/Mirai multibeam (SeaBeam 3012/2120, EM122) per cruise with open download after registration, and AUV Urashima / Jinbei / MR-X1 bathymetry as cruise products; the GODAC catalogue is searchable by cruise id and area (bbox), not by an open REST API. Pairs would again be same-cruise ship + AUV, retrievable per cruise after registration.
