Three pipeline rules had to be added during the run, each recorded in the code and re-applied to every affected pair (the pre-fix outputs were replaced, nothing else was touched):
1. **No HR mosaic coarser than native.** The June `hr_mosaic` capped the mosaic at 3000 cells across, so a dispersed MGDS release was silently resampled (MGDS:32239's three MAR sites → a 55 m "HR"; AT42-06/5174 → 14 m; FK171110 → 8 m). Now: patches are merged at native GSD when their bounding boxes cover ≥ 20 % of the union box and the mosaic is < 4·10⁸ cells; otherwise the single patch with the largest LR overlap is kept at native resolution (RC2511, EX1206, NR07-1, Amundsen, EX0909).
2. **One processing variant per dive.** MGDS Sentry releases list `_tide`, `_tide_equal`, `_autopilot`, `_gain50_navadjust` grids of the same dive; the mosaic now keeps one per dive (navadjust > plain tide > others) so different navigation solutions of one survey are never blended (NR07-1 pairs).
3. **LR grid region = the HR patch cluster the LR cruise actually surveyed.** MGDS:31813 is 16 Sentry dives across the whole Gulf of Mexico (9.7° × 2.4°), 31831 spans 6.6° × 6.9°, 33090 1.6° × 7.5°: gridding the union bounding box is impossible (10⁹ cells; the first Amundsen/SUM1004 attempts stalled at 50 GB). `grid_lr` now seeds a 5-km patch cluster with the HR part that intersects the LR cruise's NCEI footprint (largest part when there is no NCEI polygon) and grids that region only, recorded as `region_choice`.
