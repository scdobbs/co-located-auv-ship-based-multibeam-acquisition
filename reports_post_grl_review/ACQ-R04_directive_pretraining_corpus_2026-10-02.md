| phase | what | status |

| 0 | Frozen targets, evaluation and bar | closed (`phase0-freeze`) |

| 4 | Pre-registered freeze and one confirmatory scoring | later |

Round 1 showed a CNN trained from scratch on 16 labelled units reaches the bar but does not clear it. Phase 3 tests
whether an encoder pretrained on ship-only multibeam (no AUV data anywhere) changes that. This directive builds the
pretraining corpus only. The pretraining itself and the ablation run later in the CNN repo, under a separate directive.

**What the corpus must be:**
1. **Independent of every unit.** No sounding from any cruise used by any pair, and no sounding within 10 km of any HR
   footprint, development, dropped, confirmatory or lockbox.
2. **Gridded by the same recipe as the development LR**, so the pretrained encoder sees the same kind of input.
3. **Carrying the same ship products** (contract v2.1 rasters), so the V2 ship-product branch can be pretrained too.
4. **Broad:** spanning instrument families, depths and basins, so the encoder sees the range of ship data it will meet.

**Scope discipline.** Do the items below and nothing else. If a result suggests a new analysis, list it in one line
under "Not pursued". Do not run it.

**Challenge clause.** Report any wrong or underspecified instruction in §1 of the report, with evidence. Items that are
Steve's call become HOLDs (at most 3).

## 1. Exclusion set (built and committed before any download)

Build `exclusion_v1` from the inputs in §0.2. A pool cruise is excluded if **any** of the following holds:

1. **Shared cruise.** Its survey id equals the LR cruise of any row of manifest v2.1 or of the dropped list, including
   development, no-k, dropped, confirmatory and lockbox rows. Also exclude the HR cruise ids of all those rows where an
   HR cruise is recorded, and every cruise listed in ACQ-R02 §7's acquisition table.
2. **Spatial overlap.** Its NCEI footprint intersects any HR footprint buffered by 10 km. HR footprints:
   - development, no-k and dropped pairs: the pair's `footprint.geojson` on OAK, or the ACQ-R02 `hr_footprints.gpkg`
     polygon where no harmonized footprint exists;
   - confirmatory pairs (nu01a, nu01b, nu03): the ACQ-R02 `hr_footprints.gpkg` polygons only. Do not open anything
     under `harmonized_confirmatory/`;
   - lockbox pairs: as in ACQ-R01 §8, the NCEI footprints of their LR cruises (AT42-03, TN159) buffered by 10 km, plus
     a 50 km radius around each lockbox centroid from `leakage_assignment.csv`. Do not open the lockbox directories.
3. **Composite or processed product.** Anything other than a raw per-cruise survey (the ACQ-R01 §8 rule: the NCEI
   Multibeam Products layer is never used).
4. **Non-ship platform** (AUV, ROV, towed), by the ACQ-R01 §8 filter.

Use buffers computed in an equal-area or local metric projection, as ACQ-R01 §8 did (EPSG:6933), and state the
projection.

Write `exclusion_v1.json` (every excluded cruise with the rule that excluded it) and `pool_v1.csv` (every remaining
cruise with platform, instrument, instrument family, year, basin, advertised size, file formats, and depth where
known). Commit both with their sha256 **before §2 runs**. Report counts by rule, and compare the remaining pool with
ACQ-R01 §8's 3,210 cruises; explain any difference by cruise id.

## 2. Selection (seeded, committed before any download)

1. **Readable formats only.** Keep cruises whose dominant swath format the existing stage-C pipeline grids unchanged
   (ACQ-R01 §8: about 93 % of the pool).
2. **Strata:** instrument family (the ACQ-R01 §8 families) × basin (Pacific, Atlantic, Gulf of Mexico/Caribbean,
   Indian, Southern, Arctic, Mediterranean). Merge strata with fewer than 5 cruises into their family's "other basins".
3. **Sample size: 400 cruises.** Allocate a floor of 15 cruises to each instrument family that has at least 15, and the
   rest proportional to pool counts. Within a stratum, draw uniformly with `numpy.random.default_rng(20261003)`, run
   once.
4. **Size cap.** If the selected cruises' advertised raw size exceeds **3 TB**, replace the largest selected cruise in
   each over-represented stratum with the next draw from the same stratum until the total is at most 3 TB. Record every
   replacement.
5. Write `selection_v1.csv` (cruise, stratum, advertised size, file list source) and `selection_v1.json` (seed,
   allocation per stratum, replacements, total size). Commit both with their sha256 before any download.

## 3. Fetch, grid, products

### 3.1 Pilot first

Run the full chain (§3.2–3.4) on **10 selected cruises**: the first 10 in `selection_v1.csv` order that together span
at least 4 instrument families. Record per cruise: files, bytes, fetch time, grid and product time, blocks kept, output
bytes, failures.

Extrapolate to all 400 cruises. **Stop and report as a HOLD** if any extrapolation exceeds:
- raw download 3 TB, or
- 96 h wall time for the full run at the concurrency you can sustain, or
- 600 GB of persisted output on OAK.

Do not cut the selection yourself.

### 3.2 Fetch

- NCEI files only, by the `fetch_swath.py` method (byte-exact against `Content-Length`, `gzip -t`, sha256 per file).
- Raw swath goes to scratch (`$GROUP_SCRATCH/auv_ship_colocated_bathy/pretrain_raw/<cruise>/`), not OAK. Write a
  per-cruise `fetch_manifest.json` (URL, size, sha256) and persist the manifests to OAK; the raw files can be re-fetched
  from them.
- Respect NCEI: a modest number of workers (≤ 6, as in ACQ-R01), back-off on 429/503.

### 3.3 Grid by block, with the stage-C recipe

For each cruise:

1. Tile the cruise's sounding coverage into **10 km × 10 km blocks** in a local UTM projection.
2. For each block, the depth is the median depth of the soundings inside it; the cell is the stage-C documented-beam
   footprint cell, 2 · depth · tan(bw / 2), with the cruise's beam width from its metadata as in ACQ-R02 §7.
3. Grid each block with the stage-C `mbgrid` recipe exactly (`-A2 -G3 -F1 -C0 -M`, raw soundings), recording the exact
   command per block.
4. **Keep a block only if:** median depth is between 300 m and 6,500 m; grid fill is at least 50 %; and the block is
   at least 10 km from every HR footprint in `exclusion_v1` (a second, block-level guard against footprints that the
   cruise-level test in §1 could miss).
5. Write each kept block as `grid.tif` (float32, NaN nodata, positive-up, the block's UTM CRS) with `block.json`
   (cruise, block id, bounds, CRS, depth, cell, beam width, fill, command).

### 3.4 Ship products

Run the contract v2.1 builder unchanged on each kept block, with `grid.tif` in the role of `lr.tif`: the seven rasters
and `products.json`, including the §4 QA and its flags. Do not apply any frame offset. Write them to
`ship_products_v2_1/` beside `grid.tif`.

Record per block whether the products are usable by the contract consumer rule (`available` and no flags). Flagged
blocks stay in the corpus; the flag travels with them.

### 3.5 Corpus manifest

Write `pretrain_manifest_v1.parquet`: one row per kept block, with cruise, stratum, instrument family, basin, year,
depth, cell, fill, products usable (and flags), and the path to the block directory on OAK
(`.../auv_ship_colocated_bathy/pretrain_v1/<cruise>/<block>/`). Record its sha256.

## 4. Independent checks (both required)

### 4.1 Exclusion, recomputed a second way

Write a second, separate script that re-derives exclusion **at block level** from the persisted outputs only, by a
different route from §1 and §3.3:

- for every kept block, compute the geodesic distance (pyproj `Geod`) from the block's polygon vertices and centroid to
  every HR footprint polygon's vertices, without using shapely buffers;
- check the block's cruise id against the shared-cruise list rebuilt from the manifest and dropped list.

Report agreement with the §3.3 decision on every block. Any disagreement is reported with the block and both distances,
and the block is removed from the corpus manifest (a new manifest version with the change recorded).

### 4.2 Reproduction spot-check

Choose 12 kept blocks with `numpy.random.default_rng(20261004)`, stratified to cover at least 4 instrument families.
In a fresh process, from the raw files on scratch, re-grid each block and rebuild its products. Report, per block, the
maximum absolute difference of `grid.tif` and of each product raster against the persisted version. Expected: zero, or
a stated tolerance with its cause.

Write a QC figure per spot-check block (hillshade of `grid.tif`, `ship_count`, `ship_rsd`) to the report directory.
