ITEMS FOR STEERING (going into the report's challenge section; not blocking the run)
  1. Extra exclusions. I also excluded cruises within 10 km of every polygon in the ACQ-R02
     hr_footprints.gpkg: the full multi-dive HR releases, pu00 and pu03. This goes beyond the
     directive's footprint list and removes 87 more cruises (pool 2,968 instead of 3,055).
     [exclusion_v1.json:counts.excluded_only_by_extra_gpkg]
  2. Buffer distances. ACQ-R01 buffered by 10 km in EPSG:6933. That projection is not distance-true:
     at 60 deg latitude, "10 km" covers only about 5.8 km north-south on the ground. I measured true
     distances in local equidistant projections instead. Because the cached NCEI footprints are
     simplified (0.01 deg), cruises are tested at 10 km + 1.11 km; the per-block test uses exactly 10 km.
  3. ACQ-R01's "3,210 remaining cruises" counted footprint records, not cruises. Re-running ACQ-R01's
     own code gives 3,210 records = 3,016 unique cruises. Differences from this pool:
       - 153 cruises now excluded: near footprints of pairs acquired since ACQ-R01, the extra
         polygons in item 1, and the new 50 km lockbox radius.
       - 105 cruises now included: they were excluded only because of ACQ-R01 discovery candidates
         that never became units.
  4. Allocation rule. "Floor of 15 per family, the rest proportional" is ambiguous. I gave each
     family 15 plus a proportional share of the remaining 295. "Over-represented stratum" (size cap)
     is undefined; I used "share of bytes > share of cruises".
  5. Gridding projection. The development LR was gridded in geographic coordinates, then bilinearly
     warped to UTM. Pretraining blocks are gridded directly in UTM by mbgrid (same -A2 -G3 -F1 -C0 -M
     recipe), with no warp step, so they may be marginally sharper than the development LR.
  6. Depth is known for only 107 pool cruises (from the ACQ-R01 sample). The .inf files sit in NCEI
     generated/ directories, which were not walked because they are slow to list and hold no swath.
  7. Silent listing failures. The stage A/B NCEI directory walker silently skipped subdirectories it
     could not list. The ACQ-R04 crawl retries each listing and records failures instead
     (1 cruise left listing_incomplete; it is not selectable).
  8. No CHANGE_LEDGER.md exists in this repo. I will create one with the ACQ-R04 row.
