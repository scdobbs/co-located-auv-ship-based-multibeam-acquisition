## Known gaps and follow-ups

- **`lr_native_res_est_m` is None for every NCEI candidate** → `res_ratio` shows `—`. NCEI footprints carry SURVEY_ID + INSTRUMENT but not native GSD; Geoportal ISO enrichment per §4 is the optional follow-up to populate this. Without ratios the score is partially blind; ranking is still useful but the "high_ratio" flag is unavailable.
- **PANGAEA / SEANOE / ScienceBase full harvests not yet implemented.** Existing pairs are seeded from the manifest; new pair *discovery* over PANGAEA/SEANOE would require their own search-API harvesters (analogous to MGDS but with different surfaces). The directive permitted "register them as static/seed entries or query their APIs" — only the seed path is done.
- **40 MB `lr_candidates.gpkg`** is in repo. If it grows further (e.g. when more HR archives are added), per §9 it should move to `$DATA_ROOT/discovery_cache/`.

## Read-only guarantee

The discovery module only writes under `reports/discovery/` and `$DATA_ROOT/discovery_cache/`. No edit to `manifest/pairs.parquet`, no edit to any file under `$DATA_ROOT/{raw,harmonized}/`, no rerun of coreg / audit / QA on prior pairs. Tier 1 (DISCOL + 18 Cal DIG) and Tier 2 (CCZ + TAG) stay exactly as they were.
